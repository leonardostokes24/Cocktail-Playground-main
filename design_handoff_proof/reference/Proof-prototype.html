2Proof — chroma-glass, on-vision

Now working from VISION.md. Proof is an open network for cocktail R&D: invent on a private lineage canvas, cost honestly, publish to a CC-BY commons where anyone can fork — and the cross-creator fork lineage is the moat. Identity is chroma-glass: real glass material, a whisper of cyan/magenta dispersion only at the edges, backlit mono gauges, no neon. Three moments below. 2a the lineage canvas (the moat). 2b the smart radial menu (speed of capture). 2c the spec panel + costing. Type is Bricolage Grotesque / Inter Tight / JetBrains Mono.

2aLineage canvas. A classic and its forks across creators — Negroni → Boulevardier → Left Hand, plus White Negroni and Agavoni. Glass SpecNodes carry backlit GP / ABV / volume gauges; cyan→magenta edges trace ancestry; attribution + commons state travel with each spec.

1Cocktail Playground — three directions for the canvas

I read your repo: Playground is a node-graph builder — drag ingredient nodes onto a dotted canvas, wire them into a Spec node, and it auto-matches a classic (here, a Negroni). All three keep that exact interaction and your DNA (glass panels, type-colored ingredient nodes, the emerald "Branch Match" state). What changes is mood. 1a is your current dark-slate system, cleaned up. 1b re-skins it as a warm speakeasy with a real recipe-card match. 1c flips to a light "lab notebook". Same drink, same graph — pick the feel.

1aFaithful Slate. Your existing system, tightened — slate #0f172a canvas, emerald accent, glass sidebar & dock. The safe baseline everyone recognizes.
